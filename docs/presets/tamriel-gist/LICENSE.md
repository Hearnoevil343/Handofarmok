# Tamriel and Skyrim presets — map data credit

`tamriel.json.gz` (and everything built from it: the Tamriel and Skyrim recipes, the coast, lake and height
data, and the generated preset output) is derived from the Elder Scrolls Online world map hosted by
the Unofficial Elder Scrolls Pages, stitched from its map tiles at
https://maps.uesp.net/esomap/tamriel/ — with thanks to https://en.uesp.net.
