# Westeros preset — map data credit

`westeros.json.gz` (and everything built from it: the Westeros recipe, the height/coast/river
data, and the generated preset output) is derived from the fan GIS shapefiles of *A Song of Ice
and Fire* by cadaei, after the Westeros map of Tear (Cartographers' Guild) as extended by
theMountainGoat, released via the Atlas of Thrones project
(https://github.com/triestpa/Atlas-Of-Thrones).
